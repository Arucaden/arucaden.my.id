### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Community space bookings were previously coordinated through fragmented WhatsApp chats or paper logbooks, leading to double-booked facilities and lost records.
- Non-technical residents struggled with overly complex booking portals.

**The Solution:**
- Created a lightweight, mobile-first web interface with clear step-by-step reservation workflows.
- Implemented client-side reactivity using **Alpine.js** to deliver snappy calendar date picking without heavy JavaScript framework overhead.
