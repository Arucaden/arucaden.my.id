> **PresenSee** is a facial-recognition attendance system aimed at educational institutions that need more reliable check-ins than manual roll call.

### Overview

The stack pairs a Laravel backend and MySQL with Python/Dlib for face matching, and a Flutter client for on-device capture and attendance flows.

### My Role

I worked across product and engineering touchpoints: attendance UX on mobile, integration with recognition services, and validating that false positives/negatives stayed within acceptable bounds for classroom use.
