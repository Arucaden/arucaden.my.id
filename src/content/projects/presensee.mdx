### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Traditional paper roll calls and barcode scans are susceptible to proxy attendance (jockeying) and waste valuable class time.
- Standard face recognition solutions often struggle with fluctuating classroom lighting conditions and latency.

**The Solution:**
- Integrated a fast Python/Dlib face landmark comparison microservice capable of quick identity verification.
- Developed a Flutter mobile client that validates framing and lighting before submitting verification payloads.

---

### Technical Details & Architecture

- **Mobile Client (Flutter):** Built the mobile interface handling camera feeds, image compression, and secure tokenized API requests.
- **Recognition Service (Python + Dlib):** Generated 128D facial embeddings from camera frames and computed Euclidean distance matching against registered student profiles.
- **Backend API (Laravel & MySQL):** Handled attendance records, role-based access control (RBAC), and classroom schedule associations.

### Challenges & Learnings

- **Managing False Acceptance vs. Rejection Rates:** Calibrating Euclidean distance thresholds in Dlib required extensive testing across lighting conditions, camera angles, and occlusions (glasses/masks).
