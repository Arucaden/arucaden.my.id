> **Ano Galaxy Shooter** is a fast-paced 2D desktop arcade space shooter built with an intense focus on "game feel," responsive controls, and impactful visual feedback.

---

### Executive Summary & Role

As the **Solo Programmer & Designer**, I created this project to master the mechanics of tactile game feel. Rather than expanding the scope with massive levels, the focus was kept strictly on mechanical polish, visual juice, and satisfying combat interactions.

---

### Key Features

- **Tight Input Response:** Snappy 2D ship movement and weapon firing tuned for instantaneous control.
- **Boss Behavioral Patterns:** Multi-phase boss encounters powered by Finite State Machines (FSM).
- **Dynamic Feedback Loop:** Layered particle effects, screen shake, hit-stop, and damage flashing on every hit.

---

### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Typical 2D shooter prototypes often feel floaty, hollow, and unrewarding when shooting enemies lacks physical weight and visual punch.
- Complex nested state logic can easily become fragile and hard to debug.

**The Solution:**
- Engineered layered visual juice: directional screen shake, chromatic aberration, hit-stop pauses, and emission flashes to give every laser blast physical impact.
- Structured boss patterns and wave transitions using modular Finite State Machines for clean, predictable behavior.

---

### Technical Details & Architecture

- **State Machine Architecture:** Implemented simple, robust Finite State Machines (FSM) to manage enemy waves and boss attack routines cleanly.
- **Visual Feedback Pipeline:** Combined custom screen-shake algorithms with particle bursts and sprite damage flashing to emphasize impacts.
- **Post-Processing Stack:** Tuned bloom, chromatic aberration, and vignette post-processing to elevate the retro-arcade aesthetic.
