> **Magicaffeine** is a fantasy coffee-shop simulation game. Craving the familiar comfort of coffee in a magical world, you discover the local brew is dull and bitter. Determined to introduce real coffee craft, you open your own shop to spark a cultural revolution, one cup at a time.

### Key Features

- **Order Matching System:** Players select the proper cups and recipes tailored to varying customer preferences.
- **Ingredient Mixing Mechanics:** Drag-and-drop mechanics to balance and match the customer's desired stats and flavor profiles.
- **Dynamic Scoring System:** Evaluates customer satisfaction based on order accuracy, speed, and presentation.

---

### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Multiple artists with contrasting illustration styles joined the team, risking visual inconsistency.
- Some artists were unfamiliar with the technical requirements for real-time game assets (sprite padding, power-of-two resolutions, pivot points).
- Programmers were blocked from implementing core gameplay while waiting for final art.

**The Solution:**
- Created a single source of truth in **FigJam**, including strict color palettes, moodboards, and asset scale templates.
- Established a **placeholder asset workflow**, providing programmers with standardized mock sprites so they could build and test logic in parallel.
- Personally mentored team members on technical game asset constraints and streamlined the Unity import pipeline.
