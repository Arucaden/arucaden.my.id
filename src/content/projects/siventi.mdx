### Key Features & System Capabilities

| User Role | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Student / Requester** | Browse item catalog · Select quantity & return dates · Submit loan requests · Track real-time status · Due date notifications |
| **Staff / Admin** | Add, edit, or archive inventory items · Approve or reject loan requests · Update "Picked up / Returned" item badges · Review history logs |

---

### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Equipment lending across the department relied on manual, paper-based forms, leading to missing records and unclear equipment availability.
- Students lacked real-time visibility into whether lab equipment was currently in stock or checked out.

**The Solution:**
- Designed a centralized web portal with dedicated role-based views for borrowers and staff administrators.
- Built clear status workflows (*Requested ➔ Approved ➔ Picked Up ➔ Returned*) to ensure transparency and accountability.
