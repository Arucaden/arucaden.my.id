> **Damned Veil** is a dark, horror-themed pixel game set in a mysterious magical world. Combining tactical carrom-inspired physics with limited ammo, players must carefully plan geometric bounce shots to conquer challenging encounters.

---

### Executive Summary & Role

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |

| **Key Research** | Experimental 2D Pixel Art Normal Mapping & Procedural Spawner Logic (Thesis research) |
| **Core Tools** | Unity 2D, C#, Clip Studio Paint |

As **Artist and Programmer**, I created the atmospheric pixel environment, engineered the experimental 2D normal-mapped lighting pipeline, and developed the procedural spawner algorithm that powers dynamic level encounters.

---

### Key Features

- **Carrom-Inspired Combat:** Every shot counts. Players calculate geometric bounce angles against map walls to clear enemies using limited ammunition.
- **Dynamic 2D Lighting:** Integrated custom normal maps onto hand-crafted 2D pixel art to react realistically to dynamic point lights.
- **Challenging Boss Battles:** Multi-stage encounters requiring deliberate positioning, aim accuracy, and tactical resource management.
- **Hidden Secrets & Easter Eggs:** Secret geometry patterns that reward observant players with special gameplay effects.

---

### The Problem & The Solution

**The Problem:**
- Traditional 2D pixel art often appears visually flat under standard engine lighting without depth or directional illumination.
- Designing balanced, handcrafted combat encounters that remain engaging and unpredictable across multiple playthroughs is difficult for designers.

**The Solution:**
- Generated and attached custom **2D Normal Maps** directly to sprite sheets, enabling Unity's universal render pipeline (URP) light sources to create authentic 3D-like depth and shadow casting across pixel surfaces.
- Built a **Procedural Spawner System** that generates balanced, deterministic enemy compositions and placements based on difficulty curves.

---

### Technical Details & Architecture

- **Angle Reflection & Physics:** Calculated precise vector reflection angles for projectile bouncing across complex environmental colliders without clipping or tunneling.
- **2D Normal Mapping Pipeline:** Authored normal maps in Clip Studio / SpriteIlluminator and integrated them into Unity's 2D lighting system.
- **Procedural Spawning Logic:** Implemented rule-based generation algorithms to ensure fair yet challenging enemy distribution.
- **Decoupled Architecture with UnityEvents:** Replaced tightly coupled C# delegates with Inspector-driven `UnityEvent` setups, allowing UI triggers, audio cues, and particle effects to be configured without modifying core scripts.
